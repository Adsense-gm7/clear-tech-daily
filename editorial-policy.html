Standards

Editorial Policy

Originality and usefulness

We publish original explanations created for Clear Tech Daily. Each guide must solve a defined reader problem, use clear language, and avoid padding designed only to increase length or search visibility. We do not scrape, spin, or republish third-party articles.

Sources

For security, privacy, and consumer-protection claims, we prefer primary sources from government agencies, standards bodies, and service providers. Sources are linked where they materially support the guidance. Product features and prices are treated as changeable and should be verified with the provider.

Corrections and updates

We correct material errors when discovered and review time-sensitive instructions when tools or policies change. Readers can report an issue through the Contact page with the article title and relevant passage.

Advertising and commercial relationships

Advertising does not determine our editorial conclusions. Ads will be visually distinguishable from editorial content, and we will never ask readers to click advertisements. Sponsored content and affiliate relationships, if introduced, will be disclosed on the relevant page.

Scope

Our content is general education, not individualized legal, medical, financial, or professional cybersecurity advice. Readers should use qualified professional help for high-risk situations.
