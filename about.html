About us

Technology guidance without the noise.

Clear Tech Daily is an independent educational website for people who want practical, understandable help with everyday technology. We focus on digital organization, account safety, email, attention, and habits that make connected life easier to manage.

How we publish

Each guide begins with a specific reader problem. We write in plain English, avoid unnecessary jargon, and separate general education from professional advice. We do not publish scraped or lightly rewritten material. When a product or service is mentioned, readers should verify current features and terms with the provider.

Read our Editorial Policy and meet the Editorial Team.

Corrections and updates

Technology changes. We review articles when major practices change and correct material mistakes. Readers can report a problem through our contact page.

Funding

The site may use clearly labeled advertising. Advertising does not determine our conclusions, and we do not ask readers to click ads. Any future sponsored relationship or affiliate link will be disclosed where it appears.
