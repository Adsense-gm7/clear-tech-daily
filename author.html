Clear Tech Daily Editorial Team

Our editorial team creates plain-English guides about digital organization, account protection, device maintenance, and thoughtful technology use for a general U.S. audience.

Our approach

We begin with a specific everyday problem, test the sequence for clarity, and distinguish personal organization suggestions from security guidance. For security and consumer-protection topics, we prioritize primary sources such as the Federal Trade Commission and the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency.

Accountability

Each article includes its publication date. Material corrections are made promptly when identified. Our editorial policy explains sourcing, corrections, advertising independence, and the limits of our guidance.

Questions or correction requests can be sent through the Contact page.
