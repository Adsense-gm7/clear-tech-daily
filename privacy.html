Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Clear Tech Daily handles information when you visit this website.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider may process basic technical information such as IP address, device and browser type, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic logs to deliver, secure, and maintain the site. We do not currently operate user accounts or an on-site contact form.

Cookies and advertising

The current site does not set its own advertising cookies. If advertising or analytics is added, this policy will be updated before those services are activated. Third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Where required, visitors will be given appropriate consent choices through a compliant consent platform.

Google advertising

If Google AdSense is activated, third-party vendors, including Google, may place or read cookies, use web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers, and serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized ads. Visitors may manage personalization through Google Ads Settings and learn how Google uses information from partner sites. We will use a Google-certified consent management platform where required for visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland.

External links

Our pages may link to other websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Children

This general-audience site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

U.S. privacy requests

Because we do not currently create visitor profiles or sell personal information, we generally have no account data to access or delete. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide appropriate request methods. You may contact us through the method listed on the Contact page.

Policy changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the site or applicable requirements. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
